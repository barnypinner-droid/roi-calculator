How the estimate works

Most of the return comes from calls the AI resolves without an agent. We convert the agent time on those calls into seats, count only the capacity you can realistically release, and round down to whole seats. A centre cannot save 0.4 of a person, so small teams see this as a step rather than a smooth line.

We then subtract what the AI costs to run. Calls the AI resolves and missed calls it answers are billed at the AI handle time, and calls that transfer to an agent are billed for about a minute of triage. Missed-call revenue is shown as its own line, so you can judge the case with or without it.

Questions

What containment rate should I use?

It depends on your call types. Simple bookings, order status and routine account changes tend to contain well, while complaints and complex cases do not. Test a cautious figure first, then confirm it with a call-driver review.

Does this assume redundancies?

No. "Seats released" is capacity, which you might realise through attrition, less overtime or agency spend, avoided hiring, or moving people to higher-value work. The share you can realise is an input.

Why can the result be negative?

AI is not always the right answer yet. With low volumes, short calls or a small team, the running cost can exceed what you can realistically save. The break-even price shows how far pricing would have to move.

What is not included?

Telephony carrier fees, custom integrations and any uplift in customer satisfaction or out-of-hours coverage beyond the missed calls you enter.
